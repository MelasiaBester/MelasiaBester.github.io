About the Author

Melasia Bester

Melasia Bester is a multi-genre storyteller whose work explores love, identity, grief, power, survival and the complicated worlds people create for themselves.

Her growing library spans romance, fantasy, horror, thrillers, young adult fiction and supernatural stories.

From dangerous love stories to fantasy kingdoms and haunting supernatural worlds, every project begins with one goal: make the reader feel something.
