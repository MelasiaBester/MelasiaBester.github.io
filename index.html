About the author

Melasia Bester

Melasia Bester is a multi-genre storyteller whose work explores love, identity, grief, power, survival and the complicated worlds people create for themselves.

Her growing library spans more than sixty original stories, moving freely between romance, fantasy, horror, thrillers, young adult fiction, supernatural stories and more.
