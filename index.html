About the Author

Melasia Bester

Melasia Bester is a multi-genre storyteller whose work explores love, identity, grief, power, survival and the complicated worlds people create for themselves.

With 67 completed books, her growing library spans romance, fantasy, horror, thrillers, young adult fiction and supernatural stories.

Twenty-nine of her stories are currently available on Wattpad. From dangerous love stories to fantasy kingdoms and haunting supernatural worlds, every project begins with one goal: make the reader feel something.
