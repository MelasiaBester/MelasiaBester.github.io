Meet the Author

Melasia Bester

Melasia Bester is a 21-year-old multi-genre author and storyteller with 67 completed books and 29 stories currently available on Wattpad.

Her stories move through romance, fantasy, horror, thrillers, supernatural fiction, young adult stories, comedy, historical fiction, emotional drama, and genres that sometimes refuse to fit neatly into any one category.

She writes about love, grief, identity, power, survival, obsession, complicated relationships, strange worlds, and people trying to understand who they are when everything around them changes.

No two books are exactly alike. Some are soft. Some are disturbing. Some are romantic. Some are strange. Some are built to hurt a little. But every story is written with one goal in mind: make the reader feel something.

A Little About Me

The Person Behind the Stories

I’m someone who feels deeply, dreams big, and almost always has another idea waiting somewhere in my head.

My brain does not really stay in one lane. I can be thinking about a fantasy kingdom one minute, a horror concept the next, then suddenly I’m designing a business, thinking about nursing school, imagining a tattoo, planning a website, or coming up with another fictional world.

I love anime, music, tattoos, fashion, creative design, storytelling, beautiful things, eerie things, emotional things, and anything with enough personality to make me stop and look twice.

I like creating things from nothing. A title can become a character. A character can become a world. A random idea can turn into an entire finished book before I even realize how far I took it.

At the center of everything I want for my future is the same thing: I want a life that feels fully mine. Creative. Independent. Meaningful. And big enough to hold more than one dream.

Why I Write

I Started Writing the Stories I Wanted to Read

I’ve lived through grief, heartbreak, trauma, and a lot of different emotions. Sometimes you want to read something that matches exactly where your head and heart are.

But when I read, I kept running into the same problem.

I would get disappointed by the ending. I would wish a character made a different choice. I would predict the story too early. Or I would finish the book knowing there was something I wanted from it that I never got.

Eventually I started writing the versions I wanted to read myself.

At first, they were just ideas. I would start stories, get excited about them, and then move on before finishing.

Then someone I used to know told me I never finished books and called me a wannabe author.

That stayed with me.

Not because they were right.

Because I decided I wanted proof for myself that they were wrong.

So I started finishing the stories.

One finished book became another. Then another. Then another. Somewhere along the way, writing stopped being something I said I wanted to do and became something I had actually built.

Today, that catalog has grown to 67 finished books.

I write because I want people to be able to find a story for every mood. Something romantic. Something painful. Something strange. Something dark. Something comforting. Something unsettling. Something funny. Something completely unexpected.

And sometimes I want the ending to give you exactly what you hoped for. Other times, I want it to leave you sitting there thinking about it long after the story is finished.

I want the ending to make you feel something, even if it isn’t what you thought you wanted.
My Writing Style

I Don’t Believe One Writer Needs One Box

I never wanted to choose one genre and stay there forever.

Different emotions need different stories. Some ideas belong in a romance. Some need magic. Some need monsters. Some need murder. Some need humor. Some need a completely different world.

That freedom is part of what makes writing exciting to me. I can write a love story, then move into horror, then create a fantasy kingdom, then write something funny, then build an unsettling science fiction concept that starts with something beautiful and turns into something terrifying.

The genre can change completely, but I still want the emotional core to matter.

Romance & Heartbreak

Love that is soft, messy, complicated, obsessive, healing, painful, or impossible.

Fantasy & Supernatural

Kingdoms, curses, creatures, magic, power, strange rules, and worlds that feel bigger than reality.

Thrillers & Horror

Comedy & Everything Else

Satire, chaotic characters, strange premises, historical stories, emotional dramas, and whatever idea refuses to leave me alone.

What I Want Readers to Feel

I Want the Story to Stay With You

I don’t need every reader to love every character or agree with every ending.

What matters to me is that something stays behind.

Maybe you recognize yourself in somebody. Maybe a character frustrates you. Maybe an ending hurts. Maybe a world makes you wish it were real. Maybe you finish a chapter and immediately need to know what happens next.

I want my stories to feel different from each other, but I want all of them to leave some kind of emotional fingerprint.

Beyond the Books

The Bigger Vision

Writing is a huge part of my life, but it is not the only thing I want to build.

Right now, I’m focused on finishing school and pursuing a career in nursing. I want a career that gives me stability, independence, and the financial foundation to support the larger things I want to create.

From there, I want to move into real estate, beginning with an investment property and Airbnb.

The long-term dream gets even bigger.

A Creative Entertainment Destination

I want to eventually build a multi-level entertainment space that combines themed food, nightlife, books, merchandise, creativity, and community.

The vision includes a themed maid cafe, a hidden speakeasy-style lounge, and a bookstore or merchandise space where physical copies of my books can live on the shelves alongside original products and creative work.

Animation & Film

I Want the Worlds to Move

Some stories feel bigger than the page.

One of my biggest creative goals is to build an independent, Black-owned animation company with my brother.

The studio would focus on creating original animated projects from scratch, building new characters, new worlds, and original franchises.

Separately, I want to explore film and television opportunities for selected stories from my existing catalog.

I don’t want everything I create to stay in one format forever. Some ideas belong in books. Some may eventually belong on a screen. Some might become something I have not even imagined yet.
