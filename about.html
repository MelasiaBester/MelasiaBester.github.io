Meet the Author

Melasia Bester

Melasia Bester is a 21-year-old multi-genre author and storyteller with 67 completed books and 29 stories currently available on Wattpad. Her work moves across romance, fantasy, horror, thrillers, supernatural fiction, young adult stories, comedy, historical fiction, and everything in between.

She writes for readers who want to feel something. Her stories explore love, grief, identity, power, survival, obsession, complicated relationships, and the strange worlds people create for themselves.

No two stories are exactly alike, but they all come from the same place: a love of emotion, unpredictability, and creating the kind of stories she wants to read herself.

A Little About Me

More Than the Books

Melasia is someone who feels deeply, dreams big, and almost always has another idea waiting in her head.

Outside of writing, she loves anime, music, tattoos, fashion, creative design, and building ideas from scratch. She can go from thinking about a fantasy kingdom to planning a business concept to talking about nursing school in the same day.

She loves beautiful things, dark things, emotional things, and anything with a strong story behind it. Her creativity does not stay in one lane, and neither does her vision for her future.

At the center of everything she does is a desire to build a life that feels fully her own: creative, independent, meaningful, and bigger than one career or one dream.

Why I Write

Stories for Every Mood

I’ve lived through grief, heartbreak, trauma, and a lot of different emotions. Sometimes you want to read something that matches exactly where your head and heart are, but I kept finding myself disappointed by endings, frustrated by certain choices, or predicting the entire story before I got there.

So I started writing the versions I wanted to read.

At first, they were just ideas. I would start stories and leave them unfinished. Then someone I used to know told me I never finished anything and called me a wannabe author.

That stayed with me.

Not because they were right, but because I decided I wanted proof for myself.

I started finishing the stories. One became another, and another, until writing stopped being something I wanted to do and became something I had actually built.

Now I write because I want readers to find a story for every mood. Something romantic, painful, strange, dark, comforting, unsettling, funny, or completely unexpected.

I want the ending to make you feel something, even if it isn’t what you thought you wanted.

I don’t write to stay in one genre. I write to create the story I wish existed when I went looking for it.
Beyond the Books

The Bigger Vision

Writing is only one part of Melasia Bester’s long-term vision.

While continuing to grow as an author, she is focused on finishing school and pursuing a career in nursing, building the stability and independence that will help support everything she wants to create later.

Her future plans include real estate and hospitality, beginning with an investment property and Airbnb. From there, she hopes to build a larger entertainment concept: a multi-level space combining themed food, nightlife, books, merchandise, and community.

One of her biggest creative goals extends beyond publishing. Melasia plans to build an independent, Black-owned animation company with her brother, creating original animated projects from scratch while also exploring opportunities for selected stories from her catalog to become film, television, or other screen projects.

Every part of the plan connects back to the same idea: build something lasting, own the work, and create spaces where imagination can become something real.
